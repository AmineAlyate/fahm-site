Alhamdulillah meaning, word by word

Alhamdulillah (Al-ḥamdu lillāh) means “All praise is for Allah.” It is said after the obligatory prayer, and at any time, usually 33 times.

Why it matters

Questions

What does Alhamdulillah mean?

Alhamdulillah (Al-ḥamdu lillāh) means “All praise is for Allah.” It is said after the obligatory prayer, and at any time, usually 33 times.

When do you say Alhamdulillah?

It is said after the obligatory prayer, and at any time, usually 33 times.

How do you pronounce Alhamdulillah?

It is pronounced Al-ḥamdu lillāh. In Arabic: الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ.

How many times is it said?

It is usually said 33 times.
