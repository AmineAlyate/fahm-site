Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun meaning, word by word

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun (Allāhumma innaka ʿAfuwwun tuḥibbu l-ʿafwa faʿfu ʿannī) means “O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love to pardon, so pardon me.” It is said in the last ten nights of Ramadan.

Why it matters

Taught by the Prophet ﷺ to ʿAʾisha for Laylat al-Qadr, in the last ten nights of Ramadan (al-Tirmidhi).

Questions

What does Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun mean?

Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun (Allāhumma innaka ʿAfuwwun tuḥibbu l-ʿafwa faʿfu ʿannī) means “O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love to pardon, so pardon me.” It is said in the last ten nights of Ramadan.

When do you say Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun?

It is said in the last ten nights of Ramadan.

How do you pronounce Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun?

It is pronounced Allāhumma innaka ʿAfuwwun tuḥibbu l-ʿafwa faʿfu ʿannī. In Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي.
