Subhana Rabbiyal A'la meaning, word by word

Subhana Rabbiyal A'la (Subḥāna Rabbiya l-Aʿlā) means “Glory be to my Lord, the Most High.” It is said in sujud, while prostrating, usually 3 times.

Word by word

Why it matters

Usually said three times. At your lowest point, you praise the Most High.

Explaining it to a child

My Lord is perfect and higher than everything. Your head is on the floor, and Allah is the Most High.
