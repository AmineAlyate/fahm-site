Allahumma barik ala Muhammad meaning, word by word

Allahumma barik ala Muhammad (Allāhumma bārik ʿalā Muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā bārakta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd) means “O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.” It is said in the final sitting, right after the salawat.

Why it matters

The second half of the prayers on the Prophet ﷺ.

Explaining it to a child

Allah, please give lots of good to Muhammad and his family, like You gave to Ibrahim and his family. You deserve all praise, and You are so great.

Questions

What does Allahumma barik ala Muhammad mean?

Allahumma barik ala Muhammad (Allāhumma bārik ʿalā Muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā bārakta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd) means “O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.” It is said in the final sitting, right after the salawat.

When do you say Allahumma barik ala Muhammad?

It is said in the final sitting, right after the salawat.

How do you pronounce Allahumma barik ala Muhammad?

It is pronounced Allāhumma bārik ʿalā Muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā bārakta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd. In Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ.
