Allahumma antas salam meaning, word by word

Allahumma antas salam (Allāhumma anta s-Salām, wa minka s-salām, tabārakta yā Dha l-Jalāli wa l-Ikrām) means “O Allah, You are Peace and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Possessor of Majesty and Honour.” It is said right after the prayer, after Astaghfirullah.

Why it matters

Said once, after the istighfar (Muslim). As-Salām, Peace, is one of the names of Allah.

Questions

What does Allahumma antas salam mean?

Allahumma antas salam (Allāhumma anta s-Salām, wa minka s-salām, tabārakta yā Dha l-Jalāli wa l-Ikrām) means “O Allah, You are Peace and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Possessor of Majesty and Honour.” It is said right after the prayer, after Astaghfirullah.

When do you say Allahumma antas salam?

It is said right after the prayer, after Astaghfirullah.

How do you pronounce Allahumma antas salam?

It is pronounced Allāhumma anta s-Salām, wa minka s-salām, tabārakta yā Dha l-Jalāli wa l-Ikrām. In Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ، وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ.
