Subhanaka Allahumma meaning, word by word

Subhanaka Allahumma (Subḥānaka Allāhumma wa biḥamdika, wa tabāraka-smuka, wa taʿālā jadduka, wa lā ilāha ghayruk) means “Glory be to You, O Allah, and praise. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no god but You.” It is said after the opening takbir, before Al-Fatiha.

Why it matters

One of several authentic opening supplications. It is recommended, not obligatory.

Explaining it to a child

Allah, You are perfect and I praise You. Your name is full of blessing, You are so great, and there is no god except You.

Questions

What does Subhanaka Allahumma mean?

Subhanaka Allahumma (Subḥānaka Allāhumma wa biḥamdika, wa tabāraka-smuka, wa taʿālā jadduka, wa lā ilāha ghayruk) means “Glory be to You, O Allah, and praise. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no god but You.” It is said after the opening takbir, before Al-Fatiha.

When do you say Subhanaka Allahumma?

It is said after the opening takbir, before Al-Fatiha.

How do you pronounce Subhanaka Allahumma?

It is pronounced Subḥānaka Allāhumma wa biḥamdika, wa tabāraka-smuka, wa taʿālā jadduka, wa lā ilāha ghayruk. In Arabic: سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ.
