Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta meaning, word by word

Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta (Allāhumma lā māniʿa limā aʿṭayt, wa lā muʿṭiya limā manaʿt, wa lā yanfaʿu dha l-jaddi minka l-jadd) means “O Allah, none can withhold what You give, none can give what You withhold, and no one's fortune can help them against You.” It is said after the obligatory prayer.

Why it matters

The Prophet ﷺ said this after the tahlil at the end of every obligatory prayer (al-Bukhari, Muslim).

Questions

What does Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta mean?

Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta (Allāhumma lā māniʿa limā aʿṭayt, wa lā muʿṭiya limā manaʿt, wa lā yanfaʿu dha l-jaddi minka l-jadd) means “O Allah, none can withhold what You give, none can give what You withhold, and no one's fortune can help them against You.” It is said after the obligatory prayer.

When do you say Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta?

It is said after the obligatory prayer.

How do you pronounce Allahumma la mani'a lima a'tayta?

It is pronounced Allāhumma lā māniʿa limā aʿṭayt, wa lā muʿṭiya limā manaʿt, wa lā yanfaʿu dha l-jaddi minka l-jadd. In Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ لَا مَانِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَلَا مُعْطِيَ لِمَا مَنَعْتَ، وَلَا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ.

Reviewed by a qualified teacher. Other authentic wordings exist.
