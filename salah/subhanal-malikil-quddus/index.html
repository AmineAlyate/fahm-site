Subhanal Malikil Quddus meaning, word by word

Subhanal Malikil Quddus (Subḥāna l-Maliki l-Quddūs) means “Glory be to the King, the Most Holy.” It is said after the Witr prayer, usually 3 times.

Why it matters

Said three times after the salam of Witr, raising the voice the third time (al-Nasa'i, Abu Dawud).

Questions

What does Subhanal Malikil Quddus mean?

Subhanal Malikil Quddus (Subḥāna l-Maliki l-Quddūs) means “Glory be to the King, the Most Holy.” It is said after the Witr prayer, usually 3 times.

When do you say Subhanal Malikil Quddus?

It is said after the Witr prayer, usually 3 times.

How do you pronounce Subhanal Malikil Quddus?

It is pronounced Subḥāna l-Maliki l-Quddūs. In Arabic: سُبْحَانَ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوسِ.

How many times is it said?

It is usually said 3 times.
