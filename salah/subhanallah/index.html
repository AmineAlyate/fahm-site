SubhanAllah meaning, word by word

SubhanAllah (Subḥāna llāh) means “Glory be to Allah.” It is said after the obligatory prayer, and at any time, usually 33 times.

Why it matters

Said 33 times after the obligatory prayer (Muslim). You declare Allah above every imperfection.

Questions

What does SubhanAllah mean?

SubhanAllah (Subḥāna llāh) means “Glory be to Allah.” It is said after the obligatory prayer, and at any time, usually 33 times.

When do you say SubhanAllah?

It is said after the obligatory prayer, and at any time, usually 33 times.

How do you pronounce SubhanAllah?

It is pronounced Subḥāna llāh. In Arabic: سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ.

How many times is it said?

It is usually said 33 times.
