Rabbana wa lakal hamd meaning, word by word

Rabbana wa lakal hamd (Rabbanā wa laka l-ḥamd) means “Our Lord, and to You belongs all praise.” It is said standing straight after ruku'.

Questions

What does Rabbana wa lakal hamd mean?

Rabbana wa lakal hamd (Rabbanā wa laka l-ḥamd) means “Our Lord, and to You belongs all praise.” It is said standing straight after ruku'.

When do you say Rabbana wa lakal hamd?

It is said standing straight after ruku'.

How do you pronounce Rabbana wa lakal hamd?

It is pronounced Rabbanā wa laka l-ḥamd. In Arabic: رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ.
