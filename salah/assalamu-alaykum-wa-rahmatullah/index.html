Assalamu alaykum wa rahmatullah meaning, word by word

Assalamu alaykum wa rahmatullah (As-salāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāh) means “Peace be upon you and the mercy of Allah.” It is said to end the prayer, turning to the right, then to the left, usually 2 times.

Why it matters

Said turning the head to the right, then to the left.

Explaining it to a child

Peace and Allah's mercy be on you. You say it to the right, then to the left, and your prayer is finished.

How do you pronounce Assalamu alaykum wa rahmatullah?
