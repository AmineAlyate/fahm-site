La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah meaning, word by word

La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah (Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamd, wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr) means “There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is capable of all things.” It is said after the obligatory prayer, completing the hundred.

Why it matters

Said once to complete the hundred. Whoever says these after every prayer has their sins forgiven, even if they were like the foam of the sea (Muslim).

Questions

What does La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah mean?

La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah (Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamd, wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr) means “There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He is capable of all things.” It is said after the obligatory prayer, completing the hundred.

When do you say La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah?

It is said after the obligatory prayer, completing the hundred.

How do you pronounce La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah?

It is pronounced Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamd, wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr. In Arabic: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ.
