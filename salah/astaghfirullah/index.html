Astaghfirullah meaning, word by word

Astaghfirullah (Astaghfirullāh) means “I ask Allah for forgiveness.” It is said right after the salam of an obligatory prayer, usually 3 times.

Word by word

أَسْتَغْفِرُAstaghfiruI ask forgiveness ofاللَّهَllāhAllah

ArabicTransliterationMeaning
أَسْتَغْفِرُAstaghfiruI ask forgiveness of
اللَّهَllāhAllah

Why it matters

Said three times right after the salam (Muslim). Even after worship, we ask forgiveness for its shortcomings.

Questions

When do you say Astaghfirullah?

It is said right after the salam of an obligatory prayer, usually 3 times.

How do you pronounce Astaghfirullah?

It is pronounced Astaghfirullāh. In Arabic: أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ.

How many times is it said?

It is usually said 3 times.
