Dua for breaking the fast (Dhahaba z-zama') meaning, word by word

Dua for breaking the fast (Dhahaba z-zama') (Dhahaba ẓ-ẓamaʾu, wabtallati l-ʿurūqu, wa thabata l-ajru in shāʾ Allāh) means “The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills.” It is said when breaking the fast.

Why it matters

Said by the Prophet ﷺ when he broke his fast (Abu Dawud). Begin your iftar with Bismillah.

Questions

What does Dua for breaking the fast (Dhahaba z-zama') mean?

When do you say Dua for breaking the fast (Dhahaba z-zama')?

It is said when breaking the fast.

How do you pronounce Dua for breaking the fast (Dhahaba z-zama')?

It is pronounced Dhahaba ẓ-ẓamaʾu, wabtallati l-ʿurūqu, wa thabata l-ajru in shāʾ Allāh. In Arabic: ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ، وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ، وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ.
