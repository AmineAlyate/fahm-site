Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah meaning, word by word

Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah (Ash-hadu an lā ilāha illallāh, wa ash-hadu anna Muḥammadan ʿabduhu wa rasūluh) means “I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger.” It is said in the tashahhud, right after the greetings.

Why it matters

The testimony of faith, renewed in every prayer.

Explaining it to a child

I truly believe that there is no god except Allah, and that Muhammad is Allah's servant and messenger.

Questions

What does Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah mean?

Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah (Ash-hadu an lā ilāha illallāh, wa ash-hadu anna Muḥammadan ʿabduhu wa rasūluh) means “I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger.” It is said in the tashahhud, right after the greetings.

When do you say Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah?

It is said in the tashahhud, right after the greetings.

How do you pronounce Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah?

It is pronounced Ash-hadu an lā ilāha illallāh, wa ash-hadu anna Muḥammadan ʿabduhu wa rasūluh. In Arabic: أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ.
