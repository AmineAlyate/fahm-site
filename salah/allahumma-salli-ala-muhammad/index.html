Allahumma salli ala Muhammad meaning, word by word

Allahumma salli ala Muhammad (Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā Muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā ṣallayta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd) means “O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You sent prayers upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.” It is said in the final sitting, after the tashahhud.

Why it matters

Said in the final sitting. One of the authentic wordings, reported by al-Bukhari.

Explaining it to a child

Allah, please honour Muhammad and his family, like You honoured Ibrahim and his family. You deserve all praise, and You are so great.

Questions

What does Allahumma salli ala Muhammad mean?

Allahumma salli ala Muhammad (Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā Muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā ṣallayta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd) means “O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You sent prayers upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.” It is said in the final sitting, after the tashahhud.

When do you say Allahumma salli ala Muhammad?

It is said in the final sitting, after the tashahhud.

How do you pronounce Allahumma salli ala Muhammad?

It is pronounced Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā Muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli Muḥammad, kamā ṣallayta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa ʿalā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd. In Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ.
