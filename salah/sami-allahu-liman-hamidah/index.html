Sami Allahu liman hamidah meaning, word by word

Sami Allahu liman hamidah (Samiʿa Allāhu liman ḥamidah) means “Allah hears the one who praises Him.” It is said while rising from ruku'.

Why it matters

Said while rising. Allah is listening, so the answer comes next.

Explaining it to a child

Allah hears everyone who thanks Him and says good things about Him.

Questions

What does Sami Allahu liman hamidah mean?

Sami Allahu liman hamidah (Samiʿa Allāhu liman ḥamidah) means “Allah hears the one who praises Him.” It is said while rising from ruku'.

When do you say Sami Allahu liman hamidah?

It is said while rising from ruku'.

How do you pronounce Sami Allahu liman hamidah?

It is pronounced Samiʿa Allāhu liman ḥamidah. In Arabic: سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ.
