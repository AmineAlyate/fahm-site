Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika meaning, word by word

Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika (Allāhumma aʿinnī ʿalā dhikrika wa shukrika wa ḥusni ʿibādatik) means “O Allah, help me to remember You, to thank You, and to worship You well.” It is said after the prayer.

Why it matters

The Prophet ﷺ told Muʿādh never to leave saying this at the end of every prayer (Abu Dawud, al-Nasa'i).

Questions

What does Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika mean?

Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika (Allāhumma aʿinnī ʿalā dhikrika wa shukrika wa ḥusni ʿibādatik) means “O Allah, help me to remember You, to thank You, and to worship You well.” It is said after the prayer.

When do you say Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika?

It is said after the prayer.

How do you pronounce Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika?

It is pronounced Allāhumma aʿinnī ʿalā dhikrika wa shukrika wa ḥusni ʿibādatik. In Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ.
