Fahm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Fahm ("the app") helps you learn the meaning of what you say in prayer. We collect as little as possible. You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, streak, chosen goal and reminder time are stored only on your phone. Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server sends them.

Anonymous usage statistics

We record anonymous events such as "lesson completed" or "paywall shown" to understand which parts of the app help people and where they get stuck. These events are linked to a random identifier, not to you, and we do not build profiles of individuals. This is handled by PostHog (hosted in the EU).

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a technical report (device model, operating system version, app version and the error) is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. Crash reports do not include personal information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. RevenueCat helps us check whether your subscription is active, using an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipts.

Recitation audio

Word recitations are streamed from the Quran.com content network. Like any website, it receives your device's IP address when audio is loaded.

What we never do

We do not sell data, show ads, use your location, or share anything with advertisers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings at any time. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.
